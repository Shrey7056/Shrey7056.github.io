Power BI dashboard · 2026 · Personal project

Hotel Revenue & Operations Dashboard

A 3-page Power BI dashboard for a 3-hotel group, answering the questions a Director of Operations asks: how are we performing, where does revenue come from, and are housekeeping teams keeping up?

Power BIDAXPower QueryStar schemaExcel
93Kbookings analyzed
+6.9%revenue growth in 2025
28.9%Booking.com cancellation rate
$2.74MOTA commission cost

What I built

  • Cleaned the data in Power Query: removed 25 duplicate bookings and standardized 40 inconsistent segment labels.
  • Designed a star schema with two fact tables and a DAX date table.
  • Wrote 20 DAX measures (Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, YoY revenue, commission) using CALCULATE, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, SUMX/RELATED and USERELATIONSHIP.

Key findings

  • Revenue grew 6.9% in 2025, driven mainly by a higher ADR ($267.65 vs $253.94).
  • Booking.com and Expedia have the highest cancellation rates and cost $2.74M in commission.
  • Inspection pass rates fall from 93.9% to 90.6% when attendants clean more than 15 rooms a shift.

Machine learning · NLP · 2024 · M.S. thesis, University of Essex

Fake News Detection with Machine Learning

An NLP pipeline that classifies news articles as real or fake, built on a 5,000-article sample of the Kaggle Fake News dataset. I compared 11 classification models to find the most reliable approach.

PythonspaCyscikit-learnXGBoostTF-IDFSeaborn
Clean & merge text→ Lemmatize (spaCy)→ Explore (EDA)→ TF-IDF features→ Compare 11 models→ Tune top 3 (GridSearchCV)

What I built

  • Handled missing values, merged headline and body text, and removed stop words and punctuation with spaCy lemmatization.
  • Converted text into TF-IDF features and trained Logistic Regression, SVM, Naive Bayes, Random Forest, XGBoost and 6 other classifiers.
  • Tuned the three strongest models with 5-fold GridSearchCV, optimizing for precision.

Analysis

  • Compared fake and real news by article length and sentiment polarity (TextBlob).
  • Visualized the most common words in each class with word clouds and top-20 frequency charts.
  • Evaluated every model on accuracy, precision, recall, F1 and confusion matrices.
